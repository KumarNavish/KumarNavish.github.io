Continual learning / optimization ICML 2026 submission · revision

Experience Replay Through the Lens of Optimization

OpenReview ↗
Problem

Replay is effective, but often chosen by heuristic rules that hide what update it is trying to recover.

Insight

Treat replay as steering the online update toward a joint-training target under a strict batch budget.

Build

A correction target, Greedy Ball Replay, observable mismatch diagnostics, and controlled baseline audits.

Evidence

The public review record exposes both the mechanism evidence and the decisive dense-weighting objection that now shapes the revision.

Next

Make the compute–memory boundary explicit and show where subset selection earns its complexity.

Continual PEFT / geometry NeurIPS 2026 submission · revision

Rank Feasibility in Continual PEFT

OpenReview ↗
Problem

A larger LoRA rank does not automatically add the directions needed to repair old tasks.

Insight

Rank selection can be tested as a local feasibility problem over task-wise correction constraints.

Build

A minimum-norm quadratic program that either constructs a correction or exposes why the rank cannot satisfy the constraints.

Evidence

Review-stage extensions test the diagnostic across checkpoints, backbones, task orders, and simple baselines while retaining explicit limits.

Next

Separate local existence, practical optimization, and validation cost more sharply.

Time-continual language models Ongoing research

Counterfactual Window Replay for TiC-LM

Benchmark context ↗
Problem

Every historical token replayed displaces a current token, so retention cannot be evaluated in isolation.

Insight

An old window should be replayed only when it improves the temporal regret row more than the current tokens it replaces.

Build

A counterfactual objective, local replay-value estimates, uncertainty-aware allocation, and explicit no-replay decisions.

Evidence

The current stage is a bounded replication and controller study on the TiC-LM temporal evaluation structure.

Next

Measure replay half-life and test whether stable and fast-evolving domains emerge from value curves.

Agentic systems / process intelligence Live product

CasePath

Open live system ↗
Problem

Complex claim processes fail when facts, authority, required documents, and decisions drift apart.

Insight

Agentic assistance becomes trustworthy only when model output is constrained by deterministic process and evidence gates.

Build

A guarded multi-agent workflow with typed shared context, source integrity checks, live audit events, and fail-closed gates.

Evidence

The deployed workspace makes sources, process construction, documents, and final checks inspectable as one trace.

Next

Turn bounded process induction into a repeatable scientific evaluation of procedural expertise capture.

05 / Frontier

Where the body of work is heading

Intelligence should not remain trapped behind a chat box.

The next direction combines model adaptation, embodied interaction, generative environments, and interfaces that reveal rather than conceal system reasoning.

GenAI × VR / spatial computing

From generated content to generated places that can be entered, changed, and understood.

Voice or text becomes a navigable 6DoF environment; the system must reason about scene structure, interaction, continuity, performance, and human comfort—not merely produce an image.
