M.01 Revision / 2026

Continual learning · update geometry

Experience Replay
Through the Lens of Optimization

OpenReview ↗ Paper PDF ↗
01Problem

Replay spends a scarce update budget without a precise target.

Current-only learning forgets. Full joint training is unavailable. Random replay preserves examples, but does not ask whether their gradients realize the missing past-task contribution.

→
02Structural decision

Define the desired correction before choosing examples.

Recast replay as a constrained update-matching problem: the replay batch is useful only insofar as its mean gradient approaches the correction induced by the stage objective.

→
03Blueprint

Greedy subset construction with an observable mismatch.

Greedy Ball Replay selects examples one at a time, minimizing the remaining distance to the desired correction while exposing that distance at every step.

→
04Empirical proof

Matched, paired confirmation on S‑TinyImageNet / CI.

The locked 10-seed comparison measures final outcomes rather than relying on the mismatch objective alone.

M.02 Under review / 2026

Continual PEFT · geometric diagnostics

Rank Feasibility
in Continual PEFT

OpenReview ↗ Paper PDF ↗
01Problem

LoRA rank is usually selected as capacity, not as geometry.

A larger adapter helps only when the added subspace contains directions that can repair old-task loss gaps without erasing current-task progress.

→
02Structural decision

Evaluate the transition after the current task has already been learned.

Measure the old-task gaps at the actual checkpoint, project their gradients into each candidate rank, and ask whether one correction can satisfy all tasks.

→
03Blueprint

Solve a minimum-norm feasibility problem over nested LoRA spaces.

Infeasibility rejects ranks that lack a necessary direction. Excessive correction norm rejects ranks that would purchase retention by destroying current progress.

→
04Empirical proof

Local diagnosis builds a correction; held-out data decide whether it works.

The evaluation separates geometric existence from actual old-task improvement and current-task preservation.

INSCRIPTION / MINIMUM-NORM CORRECTION
δᵣ* = arg min over δ∈Sᵣ of ½‖δ‖₂², subject to Gᵣδ ≤ −Δ

The rank is not valuable because it is larger. It is valuable when its subspace first admits a sufficiently small correction that satisfies every old-task constraint.

M.04 Ongoing / 2026

Time-continual language models · replay allocation

Counterfactual Window Replay
for TiC-LM

TiC-LM benchmark ↗
01Problem

Replay is replacement, not free augmentation.

Under a fixed token budget, every historical token displaces a current token. A replay ratio therefore cannot be judged by retention alone.

→
02Structural decision

Value an old window against the current tokens it replaces.

The scientific object becomes counterfactual regret-row improvement across backward, current, and forward regions.

→
03Blueprint

Estimate matrix-valued replay effects, then allocate conservatively.

Window-specific values, uncertainty penalties, guardrails, and a no-replay solution replace fixed global ratios.

→
04Validation protocol

Predictions must appear in the regret matrix before they become claims.

This is an active investigation. The framework and falsifiable predictions are ahead of completed empirical confirmation.

INSCRIPTION / REPLACEMENT DIRECTION
Gₜ(α) = gₜ + Στ<t αₜ,τ(gτ − gₜ)

The relevant direction is historical gradient minus displaced current gradient.

PLATE F.01 / PROSPECTIVE SECTION

Generative AI × VR / spatial computing

The next interface to intelligence may be a place.

Spatial computing is not a keyword appended to the research. It is the next test of the same thesis: can a complex intelligent system expose memory, state, uncertainty, constraints, and possibility in a form a person can actually inhabit?

F.01Generative worlds

Intent → navigable 6DoF environments.

Move beyond flat generated media toward environments that can be entered, altered, and remembered.

F.02Embodied inspection

State becomes spatially legible.

Provenance, memory, uncertainty, and intervention points become objects and relations rather than hidden logs.

F.03Adaptive intelligence

The environment learns without becoming opaque.

Continual-learning diagnostics become interaction primitives inside the experience itself.

CURRENT DESIGN QUESTION

What is the smallest spatial prototype that demonstrates a genuine cognitive advantage over chat—not merely a more impressive rendering?
